About VisionAI

AI-Powered Cataract Screening Platform for Early Detection and Awareness

Our Mission

VisionAI is designed to make early cataract screening accessible through artificial intelligence. Our mission is to help people identify potential eye health risks early and encourage timely medical consultation.

Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build an intelligent web platform that assists in early cataract detection using image analysis and self-assessment questionnaires. The system aims to provide quick preliminary insights about vision health and raise awareness about cataract symptoms.

Key Features

  • AI-powered cataract image analysis
  • Vision risk self-assessment questionnaire
  • Upload or capture eye images for screening
  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Early awareness for better eye health

Technology Used

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Computer Vision for eye image analysis
  • HTML, CSS and JavaScript for web interface
  • Python based AI model integration
  • Cloud / Web-based deployment

Future Scope

VisionAI can be extended to detect additional eye diseases such as glaucoma, diabetic retinopathy, and macular degeneration. Future versions may integrate with hospital databases and telemedicine platforms to assist doctors and improve accessibility to eye care services.

Disclaimer

VisionAI is intended for educational and preliminary screening purposes only. The results generated by this system should not be considered a medical diagnosis. Users are advised to consult a qualified ophthalmologist for professional medical evaluation and treatment.
